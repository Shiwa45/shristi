What file format should my manuscript be in?

PDF is preferred (300 DPI, CMYK, 3 mm bleed). If you'd like our design service for the cover or inner pages, a high-resolution DOCX with your content is fine too.

Can I get an ISBN for my book?

Yes — select "Yes, Assign A Unique ISBN Number" in the ISBN Allocation step. This adds 5–7 extra working days to your delivery timeline.

Will I see a proof before printing?

Yes. We always share a digital proof via WhatsApp or email before going to press — production starts only after your explicit approval.

What binding options are available?

Saddle stitch, spiral binding, paperback (perfect) and hardcover. Paperback and hardcover require a minimum of 30 pages.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are printed and dispatched within the agreed timeline after proof approval. Add 5–7 extra working days if you've requested ISBN allocation.
